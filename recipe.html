Chicken Potato Soup with Bacon

Back to results
Nut allergy Winter

This thick and hearty Chicken Potato Soup with Bacon is the perfect comfort food for chilly evenings. Packed with tender chunks of chicken, creamy potatoes, and crispy bacon, this soup is a satisfying and flavorful meal. It's easy to make and perfect for leftovers, too. Serve with crusty bread for a delicious and filling dinner.

Ingredients

  • 4 slices thick cut smoked bacon, cut into shorter pieces
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 ribs celery, diced
  • 1 medium carrot, diced small or grated
  • 1 pound Yukon gold potatoes, cut into 1/2" pieces
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
  • 1 teaspoon Italian herb blend
  • 1 teaspoon dried chives
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 teaspoon chicken bouillon (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 3 cups cooked chicken, cubed or shredded
  • 1 cup half and half
  • 2 green onions, sliced on the diagonal
  • 1 cup chopped spinach leaves
  • 1 pinch red pepper flakes
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat a wide-bottom Dutch oven or soup pot over medium.
  2. Cook bacon until crispy, flipping often (about 15 minutes).
  3. Transfer bacon to a paper towel-lined plate to drain.
  4. Leave 1-2 tablespoons bacon grease in the pot; drain excess.
  5. Add onion, celery, carrot, and potatoes. Sauté until aromatics soften (8-10 minutes). Stir often to deglaze the pot.
  6. Stir in flour, mustard, and herbs. If it gets pasty, add a splash of broth. Cook 1-2 minutes.
  7. Raise heat to medium-high. Stir in broth 1 cup at a time.
  8. Add Worcestershire and bouillon (if using). Simmer 10 minutes.
  9. Crumble cooled bacon; save some for garnish.
  10. Reduce heat to low.
  11. Stir in half-and-half, chicken, bacon, spinach, green onions, red pepper flakes, and black pepper.
  12. Rest 5-10 minutes to thicken. Taste and adjust salt + pepper.
